= Stakeholder Roles for Sharing & Budget 

== Overview

This document serves as an open stakeholder catalog for the Sharing &
Budget team. Each stakeholder
group is identified, their role is defined, and their
dependency on the feature is described.


== Stakeholder Groups

=== Household Owner

*Role:* +
The household owner is the member who created the household. They are
responsible for configuring the shared budget, setting the limit
amount, the budget period, and the start date. They may also manage
household membership by inviting or removing members.

*Dependency on the feature:* +
The household owner depends on the feature to give them control over
the household's financial boundaries. They need to be able to create
and update budgets, review total spending across all members, and
maintain oversight of who belongs to the household.

*How the team accounts for them:* +
The `BudgetRemoteDataSource` supports `createBudget` and `updateBudget`
operations that serve the owner's need to configure and adjust budgets.
The household owner is the primary actor for budget setup workflows.

---

=== Household Member

*Role:* +
A household member is any user who has accepted an invitation to join
a household. They can log expenses, view the shared budget, and see
allocations assigned to them.

*Dependency on the feature:* +
Household members depend on the feature to track their contributions
to shared expenses and to understand how costs are being split. They
need transparency into budget usage and their own allocations.

*How the team accounts for them:* +
The `ExpenseRemoteDataSource` supports `fetchExpensesByUser` so that
a member can retrieve their own spending records. The allocation model
ties each expense split back to a specific user, giving members
visibility into what they owe or have contributed.

---

=== Invited User

*Role:* +
An invited user is someone who has received an invitation to join a
household but has not yet accepted. They are a transitional stakeholder they exist in the system through an invite token but have no access
to budget or expense data until membership is confirmed.

*Dependency on the feature:* +
The invited user depends on the invitation mechanism being reliable and
clear. They need to know what household they are being invited to and
what accepting means for their participation in shared budgets.

*How the team accounts for them:* +
The `InvitationRemoteDataSource` manages invite tokens that represent
pending memberships. The system must handle the case where a token
expires before the user responds, which is an identified risk in the
domain model.

*Note overlap with Household Member:* +
An invited user becomes a household member upon acceptance. These two
groups overlap at the moment of transition. The system must treat them
as distinct until acceptance is confirmed, since an invited user has
no financial access and a household member does.

---

=== Development Team (Sharing & Budget)

*Role:* +
The development team is responsible for implementing and maintaining
the Sharing & Budget features. They are a stakeholder because decisions
made during domain engineering and requirements directly affect what
they build and how they structure the system.

*Dependency on the feature:* +
The team depends on a clear domain model to avoid building a system
that does not reflect how households actually manage shared budgets.
Ambiguous concepts like how split modes are applied or when a budget
period resets directly translate into implementation inconsistencies
if not resolved at the domain level.

*How the team accounts for themselves:* +
In domain engineering, the attribute
classification, and the tangibility analysis are produced by the team
to make their own understanding explicit and reviewable.
