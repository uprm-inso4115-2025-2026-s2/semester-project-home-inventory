== #Manual Test Procedure#

#This section captures the checked-in manual procedures used to verify requirements whose verification field is *not* fully covered by automated tests in `src/test/`. Each procedure is repeatable by a reviewer who has cloned the repository, configured Supabase, and can run the Flutter client on a real or simulated device. Keeping them here (not in a separate document or external tool) makes the procedure recoverable on any tagged commit and runnable without prior team knowledge.#

=== #When to use this procedure#

* #The verification field for the target requirement says "manual tests", "usability walkthrough", or "test accounts" (see Section 2.2.3 Machine Requirements and Section 2.2.2 Interface Requirements).#
* #No automated test in `src/test/` exercises the requirement end-to-end (cross-checked against per-requirement coverage in Section 3.2.3).#

#If an automated test already covers the requirement, prefer running that test instead.#

=== #Setup#

#These prerequisites apply to every procedure unless overridden.#

==== #Environment#

* #Flutter SDK ≥ 3.35.5, Dart SDK ≥ 3.9.2 (matches MR-02 supported environment).#
* #A dedicated Supabase *test* project (never production — see "Supabase integration tests (guidance)" in Section 3.2.3).#
* #Android emulator, iOS simulator, or two physical devices when the procedure requires multi-user synchronization.#
* #An internet connection that can be toggled at the OS level (used for offline procedures).#

==== #Test accounts#

#Create the following Supabase accounts in the test project before running any role-scoped or shared-household procedure. The `test_` prefix simplifies cleanup.#

[cols="1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
| #Account# | #Role# | #Purpose#

| #`test_admin@example.com`#
| #Household admin#
| #Creates the household, invites members, exercises admin-only actions.#

| #`test_member@example.com`#
| #Member#
| #Default household member; exercises shared-item visibility and ordinary actions.#

| #`test_viewer@example.com`#
| #Viewer (read-only)#
| #Verifies that write actions are not exposed to viewers.#

| #`test_owner@example.com`#
| #Personal-item owner#
| #Owns a personal item that must remain hidden from `test_member` and `test_viewer`.#
|===

==== #Test data#

#Seed the test household with the following before procedures MP-IR-09 through MP-IR-14 (quantities and dates may be adjusted as long as relative ordering is preserved):#

* #*Shared, in-stock*: "Charmin toilet paper", quantity 24, no expiration, low-stock threshold 6.#
* #*Shared, low-stock*: "Whole milk", quantity 1, expiration = today + 5 days, low-stock threshold 2.#
* #*Shared, expiring*: "White bread", quantity 1, expiration = today + 2 days.#
* #*Personal, owned by `test_owner`*: "Barilla gluten-free elbows", quantity 3, low-stock threshold 2.#

==== #Cleanup#

#After each session, sign in as `test_admin` and delete the test household; the cascade clears items, alerts, and grocery list entries. Auth users may require Supabase admin privileges to remove; if deletion fails, log the resource IDs for out-of-band cleanup.#

=== #Recording results#

#For each run, record: date, reviewer name, device/OS, and commit SHA of the build under test; pass/fail/blocked for each step; and for failures, the actual outcome, a screenshot or device log excerpt, and a link to the tracking issue. A blank template is at the end of this section.#

=== #Procedures#

==== #MP-IR-09 — Role-Based Visibility and Permission Controls#

#*Covers*: IR-09, MR-05 (M3-QA-01, M3-QA-02). *Replaces automated test*: none. *Expected duration*: 10 minutes.#

. #Sign in as `test_owner`. Add the personal item "Barilla gluten-free elbows" with ownership marked *personal*.#
. #Sign out; sign in as `test_member`. Open the inventory list.#
+
#*Expected*: "Barilla gluten-free elbows" does *not* appear in any list, search, or filter result.#
. #Sign out; sign in as `test_viewer`. Open the inventory list.#
+
#*Expected*: The viewer sees shared items in read-only mode; Add/Edit/Delete controls are hidden or disabled with an explanatory tooltip.#
. #Sign out; sign in as `test_admin`. Open household settings.#
+
#*Expected*: Remove-member and change-role controls are visible. Confirm these controls are *not* visible to `test_member`.#

#*Pass criteria*: All four expected outcomes hold. Any visibility leak (personal item shown to non-owner, or admin control shown to non-admin) is a fail.#

==== #MP-IR-10 — Filtered Inventory Lookup#

#*Covers*: IR-10 (M3-QA-03). *Replaces automated test*: none. *Expected duration*: 5 minutes.#

. #Sign in as `test_member`. Open the inventory list with seeded test data.#
. #Apply *Stock status = LOW_STOCK*.#
+
#*Expected*: "Whole milk" appears; "Charmin toilet paper" and "White bread" do not.#
. #Clear; apply *Expiration status = Expiring* (within 3 days).#
+
#*Expected*: "White bread" appears; "Whole milk" does not (5 days out).#
. #Clear; search "milk".#
+
#*Expected*: "Whole milk" is the only result.#
. #Apply *Ownership = personal*.#
+
#*Expected*: No items appear for `test_member`. Sign in as `test_owner` and repeat: "Barilla gluten-free elbows" appears.#

#*Pass criteria*: All filters narrow results correctly and clear independently.#

==== #MP-IR-11 — Fast Manual Entry After Barcode Failure#

#*Covers*: IR-11, MR-08 (M3-QA-04). *Replaces automated test*: none. *Expected duration*: 5 minutes.#

. #Sign in as `test_member`. Tap *Add Item* and start the barcode scanner.#
. #Point the camera at plain paper for ≥ 5 seconds, then dismiss the scanner.#
+
#*Expected*: The app transitions to a manual-entry form without re-prompting for the camera.#
. #Enter only item name "Local pasta" and quantity 2; leave other fields blank; tap *Save*.#
+
#*Expected*: The save succeeds and "Local pasta" appears in the inventory list immediately.#
. #Open "Local pasta"; edit the expiration date to a future date and save.#
+
#*Expected*: The expiration date is persisted on the existing record without creating a duplicate.#
. #Repeat step 1 with an *unrecognized* barcode (a local store-brand UPC not in the database).#
+
#*Expected*: The app shows "Item not found" and transitions to the same manual-entry fallback.#

#*Pass criteria*: Both the scanner-dismissed and unrecognized-barcode paths land in manual entry with only name and quantity required.#

==== #MP-IR-12 — Budget Alert Preferences#

#*Covers*: IR-12 (M3-QA-05). *Replaces automated test*: none. *Expected duration*: 5 minutes.#

. #Sign in as `test_admin`. Open *Settings → Budget Alerts*.#
+
#*Expected*: A toggle exists for enabling budget alerts. Defaults: enabled, warning 80%, exceeded 100%.#
. #Disable budget alerts; log a purchase pushing spending above 80% of the active budget.#
+
#*Expected*: No budget alert is generated.#
. #Re-enable; log another purchase crossing the 80% threshold.#
+
#*Expected*: One warning alert appears; no second alert if a subsequent purchase keeps spending in the same 80–99% band (deduplication).#
. #Log a purchase crossing 100%.#
+
#*Expected*: A second, distinct alert is sent for the 100% crossing.#

#*Pass criteria*: Alerts honor the enable toggle and do not duplicate within an unchanged threshold band.#

==== #MP-IR-13 — Shared Alert Audience and Delivery Preferences#

#*Covers*: IR-13, MR-07 (M3-QA-06). *Replaces automated test*: integration test in `automated_check_expiration_test.dart` exercises deduplication but not delivery preferences. *Expected duration*: 10 minutes (two devices).#

. #On *device A* sign in as `test_admin`; on *device B* sign in as `test_member`.#
. #On device A, set `test_member`'s notification preference to *Grouped summary* and `test_admin`'s to *Individual push*.#
. #On device A, change "White bread" expiration to today (forces Expiring → Expired).#
. #Trigger the scheduled expiration check (or wait for the next sweep).#
+
#*Expected*: Device A receives an individual push within one sweep cycle; device B does *not*.#
. #Wait for the next grouped summary window (or trigger it via settings).#
+
#*Expected*: Device B receives one grouped summary including "White bread" alongside any other pending alerts.#
. #Trigger the same sweep again without changing state.#
+
#*Expected*: Neither device receives a duplicate push for "White bread".#

#*Pass criteria*: Delivery preferences are honored independently per member, and repeated sweeps without state changes do not duplicate notifications.#

==== #MP-IR-14 — Restock List Override Controls#

#*Covers*: IR-14 (M3-QA-07). *Replaces automated test*: none. *Expected duration*: 5 minutes.#

. #Sign in as `test_member`. Decrease "Whole milk" quantity to 0.#
+
#*Expected*: "Whole milk" is auto-added to the grocery list with a system-added indicator (e.g., a "Suggested" badge) and a reason ("Out of stock").#
. #Open the entry; use *Snooze for 24 hours*.#
+
#*Expected*: The entry is hidden from the active list. Trigger another sweep within the 24-hour window.#
. #After the next sweep, confirm "Whole milk" has *not* been re-added.#
. #Wait for the snooze window to expire (or use a developer override to advance time); trigger a sweep.#
+
#*Expected*: "Whole milk" reappears with the same reason.#
. #From the active list, *Remove* "Whole milk" outright; trigger the next sweep.#
+
#*Expected*: "Whole milk" reappears (Remove is one-shot, not a permanent exclusion), but a single subsequent sweep within the same evaluation window does *not* create a duplicate entry.#

#*Pass criteria*: Snooze suppresses re-add for the configured window; Remove does not silently re-add within the same sweep; system-added entries are visually distinguishable from user-added ones.#

==== #MP-MR-03 — Offline Operation and Conflict Surfacing#

#*Covers*: MR-03 (M3-QA-08), DR-25. *Replaces automated test*: connectivity-simulating integration tests are recommended but not yet present. *Expected duration*: 10 minutes (two devices).#

. #On *device A* sign in as `test_member`; place the device in airplane mode.#
. #While offline, add a new item "Offline cereal" with quantity 1.#
+
#*Expected*: The item appears in the local inventory list with a pending-sync indicator.#
. #While device A remains offline, on *device B* (online, `test_admin`) add an item with the same name "Offline cereal" and quantity 5.#
. #Take device A out of airplane mode and let it synchronize.#
+
#*Expected*: The conflict surface (banner, dialog, or merge view) shows both entries and offers keep both / keep local / keep remote. The local change is *not* silently overwritten.#
. #Resolve by keeping both; verify on device B that both entries are present after the next real-time push.#

#*Pass criteria*: Pending changes are marked, the conflict is surfaced explicitly, and no data is lost without user acknowledgement.#

==== #MP-MR-04 — Real-Time Synchronization Across Members#

#*Covers*: MR-04. *Replaces automated test*: none (requires two real clients on a live Supabase project). *Expected duration*: 5 minutes.#

. #On *device A* sign in as `test_admin`; on *device B* sign in as `test_member`.#
. #On device A, add an item "Sync test"; start a stopwatch when *Save* is tapped.#
+
#*Expected*: "Sync test" appears on device B without manual refresh, within the sync target window (≤ 3 seconds under nominal conditions).#
. #On device B, edit the quantity of "Sync test" to 5.#
+
#*Expected*: Device A reflects the new quantity within the same window, also without manual refresh.#

#*Pass criteria*: Both directions sync without manual refresh and within the documented window.#

==== #MP-MR-02 — Response Time Walkthrough Timing#

#*Covers*: MR-02. *Replaces automated test*: not applicable — response time is a usability measurement. *Expected duration*: 10 minutes per participant.#

. #Recruit one walkthrough participant per persona (Mateo, Sofia, Andrea, Julian — see Section 4.1 Personas).#
. #Hand the participant a phone with the build under test and ask them to complete the following while standing (simulating shopping), timing each task from first tap to confirmation visible:#
.. #Look up "Whole milk" via the search field.#
.. #Add a new item "Greek yogurt" with quantity 2 and expiration one week out.#
.. #Decrease the quantity of "Whole milk" by 1.#
. #Record the timing for each task.#

#*Pass criteria*: No participant abandons a task due to slowness; item creation (task 2) completes in under 60 seconds.#
