== Ideas

Candidate solution directions. Ideas are options to address the needs, not commitments; implementation technology choices are deferred to later sections.

=== Lightweight Smart Inventory

* Core concept: A lightweight household inventory tool optimized for very fast item capture and shared visibility in student households, so records stay current with minimal effort.
* Needs addressed: N1, N2, N3, N4, N5
* Key capabilities:
  - Fast item capture with minimal information required at entry
  - Shared household view of available items and who last updated them
  - Quick search and filtering to check availability
  - Optional, low-noise prompts to review items running low or expiring
  - Works during temporary connectivity gaps and reconciles changes when connectivity returns

* Expected benefits:
  - Reduces item-entry hassle and improves long-term inventory accuracy
  - Prevents duplicate purchases and enables smooth roommate coordination
  - Reduces the chance that people disable reminders by keeping prompts minimal and relevant

* Risks / unknowns:
  - Users may abandon the app after initial novelty wears off
  - Conflicting updates when multiple members change the same item around the same time
  - Connectivity gaps may cause mismatched inventories until updates reconcile
  - Adoption of shared features may vary

* How we will validate this idea:
  - Run quick usability sessions to see whether people can capture an item fast enough during realistic moments (e.g., after shopping)
  - Track weekly retention during pilot testing with small shared households (2-4 users)
  - Observe whether optional prompts are perceived as helpful or disruptive
