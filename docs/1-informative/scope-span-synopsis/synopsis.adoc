== Synopsis

This project addresses the inefficiencies and stress in student household management that arise when tracking essentials relies on memory or informal methods. It will produce a system-to-be that supports lightweight tracking of household items and spending for students living alone or with roommates, focusing on minimizing maintenance effort, reducing duplicate purchases, and lowering the likelihood of items expiring unnoticed. Shared-household use is supported so cohabitants can coordinate around what is available and what needs replenishing.

Work proceeds through domain and requirements engineering (including personas and scenarios), followed by architectural and design decisions and incremental implementation. Validation and verification activities (walkthroughs, scenario-based reviews, and testing) confirm that the evolving design matches stakeholder needs and that key behaviors work reliably. Across milestones, the team iteratively refines documentation and implementation based on evidence and feedback, aiming for an approach that stays practical for real student workflows over time.
