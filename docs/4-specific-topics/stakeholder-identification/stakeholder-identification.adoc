== Stakeholder Identification

This section identifies stakeholders relevant to the domain and summarizes their goals, influence, and concerns.

=== Stakeholder classification updates since M2

Since Milestones 2 and 3, stakeholder identification was updated to reflect actual project engagement. Transient household members, grocery/retail stores, and mobile platform reviewers were added as new stakeholders. Classification changes reflect observed influence and interest based on implementation work, requirements refinement, and feedback from milestone reviews.

=== 1. Primary Stakeholders (Direct Users)

These stakeholders interact with the system daily and their needs dictate the core functional requirements.

==== The Unofficial Household Manager (Persona: Andrea)

* **Who:** A frequent household supply buyer who naturally organizes the house and does bulk shopping.
* **Why:** Needs to keep the house stocked and get reimbursed quickly for shared purchases.
* **How (Relationship):** Heavy, write-intensive usage. Creates shared inventories, logs bulk purchases, and sends reimbursement reminders.
* **Functional Needs:** Shared digital ledger, rapid data entry for bulk items, verifiable records of consumption, and low-stock alerts.

==== The Budget-Conscious Roommate (Persona: Sofia)

* **Who:** A student living in a shared apartment on a strict budget.
* **Why:** Needs to track shared groceries, split costs fairly, and avoid duplicate purchases.
* **How (Relationship):** Checks the app while at the store to verify stock and logs personal vs. shared expenses.
* **Functional Needs:** Real-time inventory synchronization, duplicate purchase warnings, and separate tracking of personal vs. shared items.

==== The Busy Solo Student (Persona: Mateo)

* **Who:** A student living alone with limited time.
* **Why:** Needs to minimize time spent shopping and avoid wasting expired food.
* **How (Relationship):** Notification-driven usage; relies on reminders for low-stock or expiring items.
* **Functional Needs:** Automated expiration alerts, simple quick-check UI, and grocery list auto-generation.

==== The Health-Conscious Student (Persona: Julian)

* **Who:** A student with dietary or medical needs keeping inventory separate.
* **Why:** Needs uninterrupted supply of specialty items.
* **How (Relationship):** Precision usage with exact thresholds for reordering.
* **Functional Needs:** Adjustable low-stock alerts, strict permission/privacy boundaries.

=== Engagement channels for primary stakeholders

* Household manager and roommates identified through domain discussions and household observations.
* Budget-conscious and solo student requirements gathered from use-case discussions and workflow analysis.
* Health-conscious student needs identified through edge-case analysis focusing on dietary and medical requirements.

=== 2. Secondary & Indirect Stakeholders

Stakeholders who interact with the system occasionally or indirectly.

==== Transient Household Members (Guests, Former/Future Roommates)

* **Who:** Temporary household members.
* **Why:** Cause inventory drift or require access modifications.
* **Functional Needs:** Ownership transfer, easy user removal/invitation, and inventory reconciliation.

==== Parents / Family Contributors

* **Who:** Family members helping fund the student.
* **Why:** Want visibility into household needs and contributions.
* **Functional Needs:** Shareable grocery lists or read-only access.

==== Grocery / Retail Stores

* **Who:** Supermarkets and wholesale clubs.
* **Why:** Provide product and barcode data relied on by the system.
* **Functional Needs:** Accurate barcode scanning and fallbacks for unrecognized products.

=== Engagement channels for secondary stakeholders

* Transient members identified during edge-case analysis and lecture topic tasks.
* Parents/family contributors identified through contribution and support scenarios.
* Grocery/retail store needs identified during barcode and product metadata implementation.

=== 3. External & Technical Stakeholders

==== Development Team & Database Maintainers (INSO4115 Team)

* **Who:** Students building and maintaining the app.
* **Why:** Need clear requirements and stable backend.
* **Functional Needs:** Well-documented APIs, robust schema, and maintainable integration.

==== Project Evaluators (Course Staff)

* **Who:** Instructors evaluating the project.
* **Why:** Assess requirements coverage and project execution.
* **Functional Needs:** Documentation, traceability, and milestone feedback.

==== Mobile Platform Reviewers (App Store / Google Play)

* **Who:** App store reviewers.
* **Why:** Ensure compliance with privacy and UI guidelines.
* **Functional Needs:** Secure authentication, privacy policies, and stable navigation.

=== Engagement channels for external and technical stakeholders

* Development and database team identified through implementation work.
* Project evaluators identified via milestone reviews and feedback.
* Mobile platform reviewers became relevant during app deployment and privacy compliance checks.
