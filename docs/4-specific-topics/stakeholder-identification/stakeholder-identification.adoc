== Stakeholder Identification

This section identifies stakeholders relevant to the domain and summarizes their goals, influence, concerns, and relationship to the system.

=== Stakeholder classification updates since M2

Since Milestones 2 and 3, stakeholder identification was updated to reflect actual project engagement and requirements acquisition. Transient household members, grocery/retail stores, and technical maintainers were refined as stakeholders based on implementation reviews, milestone feedback, backend integration, and acquisition sessions, and classifications were updated to better document each stakeholder's influence and interest.

=== 1. Primary Stakeholders (Direct Users)

These stakeholders interact with the system daily, and their needs dictate the core functional requirements.

==== The Unofficial Household Manager (Persona: Andrea)

* **Who:** A frequent household supply buyer who organizes the house and does bulk shopping.
* **Why:** Keep the house stocked and get reimbursed quickly for shared purchases.
* **How:** Heavy, write-intensive usage — creates shared inventories, logs bulk purchases, sends reimbursement reminders.
* **Interest / Influence:** High / High
* **Functional Needs:** Shared digital ledger, rapid bulk data entry, verifiable consumption records, low-stock alerts.
* **Traceability:** M3-QA-06, M3-QA-07 (shared alert coordination, inventory planning, restock workflows).

==== The Budget-Conscious Roommate (Persona: Sofia)

* **Who:** A student in a shared apartment on a strict budget.
* **Why:** Track shared groceries, split costs fairly, and avoid duplicate purchases.
* **How:** Checks the app at the store to verify stock and logs personal vs. shared expenses.
* **Interest / Influence:** High / Medium
* **Functional Needs:** Real-time inventory sync, duplicate purchase warnings, separate personal/shared tracking.
* **Traceability:** M3-QA-05 (budget tracking, spending entries, threshold management).

==== The Busy Solo Student (Persona: Mateo)

* **Who:** A student living alone with limited time.
* **Why:** Minimize shopping time and avoid wasting expired food.
* **How:** Notification-driven; relies on reminders for low-stock or expiring items.
* **Interest / Influence:** High / Medium
* **Functional Needs:** Automated expiration alerts, simple quick-check UI, grocery list auto-generation.
* **Traceability:** M3-QA-07 (restock planning and low-stock workflows).

==== The Health-Conscious Student (Persona: Julian)

* **Who:** A student with dietary or medical needs keeping inventory separate.
* **Why:** Maintain an uninterrupted supply of specialty items.
* **How:** Precision usage with exact reorder thresholds.
* **Interest / Influence:** High / Medium
* **Functional Needs:** Adjustable low-stock alerts, strict permission/privacy boundaries.
* **Traceability:** M3-QA-01, M3-QA-02 (visibility scope, ownership boundaries, role-scoped permissions for personal items).

=== Engagement channels for primary stakeholders

* Household manager and roommate requirements: acquisition discussions, shared-apartment usage scenarios, milestone feedback.
* Budget-conscious and solo student requirements: implementation reviews, inventory tracking discussions, grocery scenarios, feature refinement.
* Health-conscious student requirements: edge-case acquisition sessions on dietary restrictions, privacy, and specialized tracking.

=== 2. Secondary & Indirect Stakeholders

Stakeholders who interact with the system occasionally or indirectly.

==== Transient Household Members (Guests, Former/Future Roommates)

* **Who:** Temporary household members.
* **Why:** Cause inventory drift or require access modifications.
* **Interest / Influence:** Low / Medium
* **Functional Needs:** Ownership transfer, easy user removal/invitation, inventory reconciliation.
* **Traceability:** M3-QA-02 (membership handling and role-scoped permissions).

==== Parents / Family Contributors

* **Who:** Family members helping fund the student.
* **Why:** Want visibility into household needs and contributions.
* **Interest / Influence:** Medium / Low
* **Functional Needs:** Shareable grocery lists or read-only access.
* **Traceability:** Grocery contribution and household support acquisition scenarios.

==== Grocery / Retail Stores

* **Who:** Supermarkets and wholesale clubs.
* **Why:** Provide product and barcode data the system relies on.
* **Interest / Influence:** Medium / Medium
* **Functional Needs:** Accurate barcode scanning and fallbacks for unrecognized products.
* **Traceability:** M3-QA-04 (barcode lookup fallback and product metadata acquisition).

=== Engagement channels for secondary stakeholders

* Transient member requirements: shared inventory ownership discussions and access-management reviews.
* Parents/family contributor needs: grocery contribution and household support acquisition scenarios.
* Grocery/retail store requirements: barcode integration reviews, product metadata discussions, implementation testing.

=== 3. External & Technical Stakeholders

==== Development Team & Technical Maintainers (INSO4115 Team)

* **Who:** Students building and maintaining the app.
* **Why:** Need clear requirements, maintainable architecture, and stable backend functionality.
* **Interest / Influence:** High / High
* **Functional Needs:** Well-documented APIs, robust schema, maintainable integrations, stable backend services.
* **Traceability:** M3-QA-08 (synchronization handling, offline queue management, backend integration).

==== Project Evaluators (Course Staff)

* **Who:** Instructors evaluating the project.
* **Why:** Assess requirements coverage, documentation quality, and execution.
* **Interest / Influence:** High / High
* **Functional Needs:** Documentation, traceability, milestone evidence, clear requirement justification.
* **Traceability:** Milestone feedback, review comments, documentation evaluation.

=== Engagement channels for external and technical stakeholders

* Development team and maintainer requirements: implementation reviews, backend integration, milestone planning, schema maintenance discussions.
* Project evaluator requirements: milestone reviews, QA feedback, documentation assessment.

=== 4. Potential Future Stakeholders

==== Mobile Platform Reviewers (Future Deployment Stakeholder)

* **Who:** App Store and Google Play reviewers.
* **Why:** Would evaluate the application if distributed through official marketplaces.
* **Interest / Influence:** Low / High
* **Functional Needs:** Secure authentication, privacy policies, stable navigation, compliance with platform requirements.

These stakeholders become relevant if the application is later distributed through official marketplaces such as the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
