== Personas

Personas represent key user/stakeholder types and are the single source of truth for all requirements, user stories, and features. Each includes goals, frustrations, behaviors, constraints, and a short scenario. The persona-to-needs trace table at the end links each persona to specific needs (N1, N2, etc.) referenced throughout requirements for justification and traceability.

=== Persona 1: Mateo, The Busy Solo Student
* *Role:* Student living alone
* *Background:* A full-time engineering student who also works part-time, lives alone in a small apartment near campus, and has little free time or mental energy for household management.
* *Goals:*
** Minimize time spent grocery shopping.
** Avoid throwing away expired perishable food.
** Keep meal prep quick, simple, and predictable.
* *Frustrations:*
** Frequently forgets what ingredients he already has.
** Buys produce or meat that goes bad before he cooks it.
** Morning stress when he realizes he is out of coffee or milk.
* *Typical behaviors:*
** Shops sporadically, usually only when the fridge is empty.
** Relies entirely on memory for his inventory.
** Orders expensive takeout when overwhelmed by a disorganized kitchen.
* *Constraints:*
** Severe time constraints from a heavy class and work schedule.
** Limited mental bandwidth; relies on his smartphone for organization.
* *Scenario:*
Mateo comes home late and plans to pan-fry chicken he bought earlier in the week, only to find he actually bought it a week and a half ago and it has spoiled. He orders expensive pizza (breaking his budget) and later finds three open bottles of ketchup in the pantry. Success looks like an alert (delivery TBD / in-app baseline) the day before his chicken expires, and quickly checking the app at the store to confirm he doesn't need a fourth ketchup bottle.

=== Persona 2: Sofia, The Budget-Conscious Roommate
* *Role:* Student living with roommates; budget-conscious
* *Background:* Lives in a shared apartment with two other students and pays her own expenses using financial aid and a strict monthly budget.
* *Goals:*
** Keep grocery and household supply costs as low as possible.
** Ensure communal items like toilet paper and dish soap are split fairly.
** Avoid buying duplicates of items the apartment already has.
* *Frustrations:*
** Roommates using her more expensive dietary items without asking.
** Running out of money before month-end due to poor collective planning.
** Wasting limited storage space on duplicate bulk items.
* *Behaviors:*
** Shops with a strict list and a calculator app open.
** Uses a fridge whiteboard for shared needs, but roommates forget to update it.
* *Constraints:*
** Extremely limited budget; shared storage that fills up quickly; reliant on uncooperative roommates to update the whiteboard.
* *Scenario:*
Sofia shops on a strict $50 budget, checks the shared whiteboard, and buys toilet paper and dish soap. On returning she finds a roommate had already bought bulk toilet paper that afternoon but forgot to erase it — leaving no storage space and Sofia short on cash. Success looks like a synchronized app where changes are recorded so everyone sees updated shared inventory and purchases, preventing duplicate spending.

=== Persona 3: Julian, The Health-Conscious Student
* *Role:* Student with dietary or medical needs
* *Background:* A student athlete on a strict high-protein, gluten-free diet who lives with roommates but keeps his food separate due to nutritional requirements and cross-contamination concerns.
* *Goals:*
** Maintain a consistent, uninterrupted supply of specialized dietary items.
** Track consumption rates of expensive supplements to budget for reorders.
* *Frustrations:*
** Running out of core meal-prep ingredients mid-week.
** Disorganized pantry spaces making items hard to find.
** Specialty items expiring after bulk online purchases.
* *Behaviors:*
** Buys non-perishables in bulk online; does heavy meal prep every Sunday.
** Tracks macronutrients religiously but struggles to translate that into a grocery inventory.
* *Constraints:*
** Strict dietary policies with no substitutions; limited local availability of his brands.
* *Scenario:*
During Sunday meal prep, Julian reaches for his special gluten-free pasta and finds he used the last box on Wednesday and forgot to reorder; since the local store doesn't carry his brand, his meal plan is ruined. Success looks like threshold-based, adjustable low-stock alerts that remind him to reorder specialty items before he runs out.

=== Persona 4: Andrea, The Unofficial Household Manager
* *Role:* Frequent household supply buyer; roommate who shares purchases
* *Background:* Lives with three other students and, being the most organized and the one with a car, became the "unofficial house manager," doing the bulk of shopping at wholesale clubs.
* *Goals:*
** Keep the house stocked with shared essentials without all the mental labor.
** Get reimbursed quickly and accurately by roommates.
* *Frustrations:*
** Constantly texting the group chat about what they're out of, getting no replies.
** Getting stuck paying when shared items are consumed but nobody admits to finishing them.
* *Behaviors:*
** Uses a chaotic mix of WhatsApp, scattered receipts, and Apple Notes to track needs.
** Frequently sends money requests after a shopping trip.
* *Constraints:*
** Coordinates across three uncooperative roommate schedules; needs unarguable proof of consumption for reimbursements.
* *Scenario:*
Andrea plans a wholesale-club trip, texts roommates, and gets one reply; she guesses on the rest. The shared snacks disappear fast and arguments break out over who ate them, while the house runs out of trash bags she assumed they had. Success looks like a transparent shared digital ledger where roommates log what they finish and request items, making reimbursements unarguable and keeping the house stocked.
