== Domain Logic for Spending Tracking

Spending tracking represents how household purchases are recorded, attributed, and preserved over time. A spending event exists only when a financial transaction occurs within a household context, and it supports later budgeting, reporting, and accountability without depending on any specific implementation.

A spending event captures:
* who paid
* what was purchased
* the monetary amount
* when the transaction occurred
* whether the cost is personal or shared

=== Core rules

* Spending is recorded only when a purchase with a defined cost exists; creating or editing non-financial item information does not by itself create spending.
* Spending is always associated with a household context and does not exist independently of it.
* Every spending event must identify a payer; anonymous financial records are not allowed.
* Spending must distinguish personal from shared attribution, and shared attribution does not remove payer identification.
* Spending records are historical and must remain traceable; deleting or changing inventory items does not erase past spending.
* Spending depends on a valid purchased item or recorded purchase context; orphaned financial records are not valid.
* Corrections to cost information must preserve traceability through a new entry or other explicit record of change rather than silently overwriting history.

=== Implications

These rules keep spending tied to household activity and preserve accountability, supporting later budgeting, reporting, and reimbursement logic without redefining the core domain concepts.
